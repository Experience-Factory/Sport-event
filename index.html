Europa League / Conference League: when a Belgian club's match is broadcast free on Belgian public TV (RTBF / RTL, VRT / VTM), it is added here; these are decided close to the date, so check the current week.

Good to know: the F1 Las Vegas GP is a night race, shown here at ~05:00 Sunday morning. The Vuelta has two rest days (usually Mondays) and the PDC World Championship pauses for Christmas (24–26 Dec) — those days may be blank. During the Hockey World Cup, matches take place almost every day (15–30 Aug); the exact group games listed are Belgium's — the knockout matches from 24 Aug depend on results.
